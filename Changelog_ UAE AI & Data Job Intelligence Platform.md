# Changelog: UAE AI & Data Job Intelligence Platform

## 1. Introduction

This Changelog provides a chronological record of all significant changes made to the UAE AI & Data Job Intelligence Platform. It details new features, bug fixes, improvements, and any other modifications that impact the functionality or architecture of the project. This document serves as a vital historical reference for understanding the platform's evolution and is crucial for effective release management, debugging, and communicating updates to all stakeholders.

## 2. Audience

This document is a key resource for both our **human developers** and **AI coding agents**. Human developers will find it invaluable for quickly understanding recent changes, assisting in debugging efforts, and coordinating development. AI coding agents will use it to understand the history of changes, which can inform their code generation and refactoring decisions, ensuring they work with the most up-to-date project state. Ultimately, it acts as a central record of project evolution, fostering clear communication and alignment across all contributors.

## 3. Release History

### 0.1.0 - 2026-07-02

#### Added

This initial release establishes the foundational project setup and repository structure. We've laid down the core documentation and planning foundation, including:

*   **`PROJECT_VISION.md`**: Defining our mission, target users, success metrics, and project non-goals.
*   **`PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`**: Outlining both functional and non-functional requirements for the platform.
*   **`ARCHITECTURE.md`**: Describing the high-level system architecture, component responsibilities, and communication flows.
*   **`TECH_STACK.md`**: Documenting our chosen technologies and development tools.
*   **`DATA_SOURCES.md`**: Identifying initial data sources and our collection strategy.
*   **`DATA_MODEL.md`**: Defining the conceptual data model with its fact and dimension tables.
*   **`PIPELINE_DESIGN.md`**: Detailing the data pipeline components and their flow.
*   **`AI_STRATEGY.md`**: Outlining our AI objectives, technologies, and integration points.
*   **`ROADMAP.md`**: Presenting the product roadmap, including MVP, V1, V2, and stretch goals.
*   **`SPRINT_01.md`**: Defining the tasks and deliverables for our first 7-day sprint.
*   **`TASKS.md`**: Providing a structured format and examples for atomic implementation tasks.
*   **`DECISIONS.md`**: Initiating our Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) with key decisions on Docker Compose, PostgreSQL, DuckDB, dbt Core, Prefect, FastAPI, Streamlit, Ollama, Qwen/Gemma, Ruff, mypy, pytest, and pre-commit.
